Questions that help you compare options

Use these prompts when talking with an installer. You don’t need to know every technical detail before you call, but you should feel comfortable asking what happens next.

  • What details about my room do you need before suggesting a plan?
  • Which parts of the project are included, from planning through finishing?
  • How will the simulator equipment be integrated with the space?
  • Can the design account for golf along with another activity, such as racing or a different sport?
  • What should I prepare before the work begins, and what decisions will I need to make?
  • How will changes or questions be discussed if the room plan needs adjustment?

Watch for a mismatch between what you’ve asked for and what is described in the proposed scope. Don’t choose a plan just because it sounds comprehensive. Make sure you understand what the work covers and how it relates to your room.
